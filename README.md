### 🌍 Kabir AI — Your AI Travel Companion

**An AI-powered travel companion that helps travelers explore destinations, discover local experiences, access assistance, and preserve their memories.**

Kabir brings together **conversational AI, location-aware context, destination knowledge, and personalized travel services** to create an intelligent and interactive travel experience.

### ✨ Key Features

**🤖 Context-Aware AI Companion**
- Natural-language conversations powered by LLMs.
- Destination-specific assistance and historical storytelling.
- Contextual responses based on the traveler's location and journey.
- Multilingual conversational experiences.

**🔎 Intelligent Knowledge Retrieval**
- Semantic search using vector embeddings and Pinecone.
- Retrieval of relevant destination-specific knowledge.
- Conversation history and location context management.
- Contextual knowledge integration with LLM-generated responses.

**📍 Location-Aware Travel Experiences**
- Monument and destination discovery.
- Location-specific information and travel guidance.
- Nearby attractions, local experiences, and recommendations.

**🛎️ Travel & Concierge Services**
- Assistance with local guides, photographers, and travel-related services.
- Service discovery and booking workflows.
- AI-assisted travel support.

**📸 AI-Powered Travel Memories**
- Travelers can share conversations, photographs, and experiences.
- Personalized travel stories and digital postcards generated from their journey.
